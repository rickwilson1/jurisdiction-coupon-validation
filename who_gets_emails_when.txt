Team,

When you place a test order using one of the OCWR coupon codes, here's exactly what the new automated dispatch system will send and to whom. All emails come from dispatch@agromin.com.


===========================================================
EVERY ORDER — CUSTOMER GETS AN EMAIL
===========================================================

The customer who placed the order receives a confirmation email with instructions specific to their order type:

  • Pickup, less than 5 cubic yards
    → "Self-loading pickup" instructions: bring buckets, ID, and a vehicle.
       Includes the assigned yard's address, phone, and hours.

  • Pickup, 5 cubic yards or more
    → "Staff-loaded pickup" instructions: customer must bring a truck or
       trailer with solid sides; OCWR staff loads with heavy equipment.

  • Delivery
    → "Delivery confirmation": acknowledges the order and tells the
       customer Agromin will contact them within 1 business day to
       schedule.

Ofelia (ofelia.velarde-garcia@ocwr.ocgov.com) is automatically CC'd on
every customer email.

Kendall (kendall@agromin.com) is automatically BCC'd on every customer
email, so she sees exactly what the customer received. BCC rather than CC
keeps Agromin addresses off the header the customer sees.

On delivery orders, Greg Jackson is also CC'd on the customer email —
the delivery copy tells the customer "I am CC'ing Greg Jackson," so he is
a real CC and not only a recipient of the internal alert below.


===========================================================
DELIVERY ORDERS ONLY — COORDINATORS GET AN ALERT
===========================================================

If the order is a delivery, a SECOND email is sent — separately from the
customer email — to the OCWR delivery coordinators, with the customer's
name, phone, address, material, and quantity. This is the "please call
this customer to schedule" alert.

Recipients:
  • Greg Jackson  (greg@agromin.com)
  • Brian         (brian@agromin.com)
  • Kendall       (kendall@agromin.com)

Coordinator alerts are NOT CC'd to the customer or to Ofelia — only the
three named coordinators see them.


===========================================================
QUICK REFERENCE
===========================================================

Order type        Customer   Ofelia CC   Greg CC   Kendall BCC   Coordinator alert
-----------       --------   ---------   -------   -----------   -----------------
Pickup (any size)   Yes        Yes         No        Yes           No
Delivery            Yes        Yes         Yes       Yes           Greg, Brian, Kendall


===========================================================
WHAT CUSTOMERS SEE
===========================================================

The "From" address on every email is dispatch@agromin.com (a dedicated
shared mailbox). Replies do not go there alone. Every customer email
carries a Reply-To naming the person who owns follow-up, with
dispatch@agromin.com alongside so the thread stays visible to everyone
with mailbox access:

  Pickup (any size)   Kendall + dispatch
  Delivery, OC        Greg + dispatch
  Delivery, Ventura   Chris + dispatch
  Delivery, Sac       Rosa + dispatch

The delivery Reply-To is the same coordinator the email names as
following up, so a reply reaches the person the customer was just told
to expect.

Ofelia is CC'd on every customer email and sees the full thread, but she
is the OCWR escalation path, not the first responder. County staff are
not the default front line for Agromin's customer mail.


===========================================================
NOT IN SCOPE FOR THIS ROLLOUT
===========================================================

Ventura County and Sacramento coupon codes are in the system but are
NOT being tested in this round. The OCWR program is the only active
rollout right now. When Ventura and Sacramento launch, their delivery
alerts will go to Chris (Ventura) and Rosa (Sacramento) — different
recipients from OCWR.


Reply to this email if anything looks off after your test orders.
