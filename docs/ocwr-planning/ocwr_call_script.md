## Q&A cheat sheet

**"What does the customer email say?"**
> "Pickup orders get yard address, hours, and a QR code note. Delivery orders get a 1-business-day callback promise, then a second email when Greg confirms the date. Ofelia CC'd on all."

**"What if the coupon is invalid?"**
> "CIMcloud rejects it at checkout — we never see it."

**"What if a resident replies to the email?"**
> "Replies go to dispatch@agromin.com — Greg and Brian have access."

**"Can we see the data?"**
> "Yes — SharePoint Excel from day one. Phase 2 adds fulfillment data on top."
